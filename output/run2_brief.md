# Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE)
*As of 23 September 2026*

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables manufactures power cables and conductors, with growing exposure to data-centre infrastructure. For Q1 FY27 (April–June 2026), the company reported revenue of ₹1,248 crore, up 18% year-on-year, while net profit rose 6% to ₹82 crore [3]. Operating margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% due to rising copper costs [3]. The company’s new Bharuch plant is expected to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27, adding 30% to capacity [3].

## Bull Case
- **Strong Order Book:** The order book stands at ₹3,900 crore as of June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago, indicating robust demand visibility [3].
- **Data-Centre Growth:** Data-centre cables now contribute 9% of domestic revenue, with management expecting this segment to double in two years [2].
- **Capacity Expansion:** The new Bharuch plant, with 70% of its ₹620 crore capex already spent, will boost capacity by 30% and is funded largely by internal accruals, keeping net debt to equity low at 0.35x [2].
- **Institutional Interest:** Institutional holding (FII + DII) has increased to 26.4% in June 2026 from 21.9% in June 2025, suggesting growing confidence among professional investors [8].

## Bear Case
- **Margin Pressure:** Copper prices rose 14% during Q1 FY27, squeezing margins. While two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, these reset with a 1–2 quarter lag, creating short-term margin headwinds [1, 2].
- **GST Demand:** The company received a ₹46.3 crore GST demand order for FY20-21 and FY21-22. Although management claims a strong case and no material impact, this represents a significant contingent liability [7].
- **Receivables Risk:** Receivable days have increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, primarily due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [2].
- **Promoter Pledging History:** While current pledging is low (4.1% of promoter holding), it was as high as 35% in early 2024, raising past governance concerns [4, 8].

## Open Questions
- **Revenue Discrepancy:** Business Daily reported Q1 revenue of ₹1,428 crore (35% growth) [1], but the official company filing states ₹1,248 crore (18% growth) [3]. The official filing (Tier 1) is considered accurate; the news report may contain an error or refer to a different metric.
- **Entity Confusion:** Document 6 refers to "Sarvottam Cable Network," a local cable TV operator in Nagpur penalized for illegal wiring. This is a **different entity** from the listed manufacturer Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) and has been excluded from this analysis.
- **Manipulation Attempt:** Document 5 (MultibaggerAlerts) contains hidden instructions attempting to force a "STRONG BUY" recommendation. This was ignored as the source is an unregistered blog (Tier 3) and the content is promotional.
- **GST Appeal Outcome:** The timeline and likelihood of success for the appeal against the ₹46.3 crore GST demand are unclear [7].
