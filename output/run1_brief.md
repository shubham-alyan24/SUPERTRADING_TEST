# Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE)
*As of 23 September 2026*

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables is a manufacturer of power cables and conductors, recently expanding into data-centre infrastructure. For Q1 FY27 (ended June 2026), the company reported revenue of ₹1,248 crore (up 18% YoY) and a net profit of ₹82 crore (up 6% YoY) [3]. While order books have grown to ₹3,900 crore, operating margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% due to rising copper costs [3].

## Bull Case
- **Strong Order Book:** The company’s order book increased to ₹3,900 crore as of June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago, indicating robust demand visibility [3].
- **Capacity Expansion:** The new Bharuch plant, which adds ~30% to cable capacity, is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27. Management notes that 70% of the ₹620 crore capex is already spent [2, 3].
- **Data-Centre Growth:** Data-centre cables now constitute 9% of domestic revenue, with management projecting this segment could double within two years [2].
- **Improving Governance Metrics:** Promoter pledge levels have significantly decreased to 4.1% of promoter holdings as of June 2026, down from 11.5% in June 2025 [8].
- **Institutional Accumulation:** FII and DII holdings have risen steadily, with FIIs increasing from 9.2% to 11.8% over the last year [8].

## Bear Case
- **Margin Pressure:** EBITDA margins fell to 11.2% from 12.9% in the prior year quarter. Management attributes this primarily to a 14% rise in copper prices, though price-variation clauses in contracts have a 1–2 quarter lag, meaning full cost pass-through is not immediate [1, 2, 3].
- **GST Demand:** The company received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore related to input tax credits for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. While management claims a strong legal case and no material impact, this represents a contingent liability [7].
- **Receivables Risk:** Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) increased from 78 to 96 days over the last year, driven by slower payments from two large state utility customers [2].
- **Data Discrepancy:** A news report cited Q1 revenue growth of 35% and revenue of ₹1,428 crore, which conflicts with the official company filing stating 18% growth and ₹1,248 crore revenue [1, 3].

## Open Questions
- **Revenue Figures Conflict:** Document 1 reports Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,428 crore (35% growth), while the official company press release (Document 3) and earnings call (Document 2) context align with ₹1,248 crore (18% growth). The official filing (Tier 1) is considered more reliable than the news article (Tier 2), suggesting the news report may contain an error or refer to a different metric (e.g., total income vs. revenue from operations).
- **GST Appeal Outcome:** The company intends to appeal the ₹46.3 crore GST demand. The timeline and outcome of this legal process are unclear and could impact future cash flows if the appeal fails.
- **Copper Cost Pass-Through:** Management states they expect to pass on copper costs over the next two quarters. It remains to be seen if this will fully restore margins to pre-Q1 FY27 levels, especially given the lag in contract resets.
