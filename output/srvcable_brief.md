# Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd. (NSE: SRVCABLE)  
*As of 23 September 2026*  

## Snapshot  
Sarvottam Cables Ltd. manufactures power cables, wires and conductors for utilities, infrastructure and data‑centre projects. Q1 FY27 (Apr‑Jun 2026) revenue rose 18% YoY to **₹1,248 cr** with EBITDA margin slipping to **11.2%** due to a sharp rise in copper prices【3】. The company’s order book is now **₹3,900 cr**, and a new Bharuch plant (adding ~30% capacity) is slated for commercial production in Q3 FY27【3】. Net debt‑to‑equity stands at **0.35×**【2】.

## Bull Case  
- **Strong demand tailwinds** – Power‑transmission and data‑centre projects are driving order growth; data‑centre cables already represent ~9% of domestic revenue and could double in two years【2】.  
- **Robust order book** – At ₹3,900 cr, the order backlog provides visibility for revenue growth through FY28【3】.  
- **Capacity expansion** – The Bharuch plant will boost output by ~30% and is funded largely by internal accruals, limiting dilution【2】.  
- **Improving export mix** – Export contribution rose to 21% of revenue, diversifying away from domestic utility cycles【3】.  
- **Healthy balance sheet** – Net debt‑to‑equity of 0.35× and modest leverage suggest capacity to absorb short‑term cost pressures【2】.  

## Bear Case  
- **Copper price volatility** – A 14% rise in copper cost compressed margins by 170 bps in Q1; the company can only partially pass on the increase due to lagged price‑variation clauses【1】【3】.  
- **Rising receivables** – Days sales outstanding climbed from 78 to 96 days, reflecting slower payments from large state utilities, which could strain cash flow if defaults emerge【2】.  
- **GST demand order** – A ₹46.3 cr demand (including interest/penalty) for input‑tax credit issues has been disclosed; while management says impact will be minimal, it adds legal risk【7】.  
- **Promoter pledge perception** – Earlier media reported a 35% promoter pledge (2024) raising governance concerns【4】, but the latest shareholding filing shows pledging at **4.1% of promoter holdings**【8】. The discrepancy may cause investor uncertainty.  
- **Execution risk at new plant** – Commercial production is expected in Q3 FY27; any delays could postpone capacity‑related revenue upside.  

## Open Questions  
- **Margin outlook:** How quickly can Sarvottam fully pass on higher copper costs, and will competitive pricing pressure intensify?  
- **Receivables management:** What concrete steps are being taken to reduce the DSO trend and mitigate exposure to utility payment delays?  
- **GST litigation:** What is the likely timeline and potential financial impact of the GST demand appeal?  
- **Promoter pledge data:** Why did the 2024 news article cite a 35% pledge while the 2026 filing shows only 4.1%? Is the earlier figure a misinterpretation or has the pledge been dramatically reduced?  
- **Plant commissioning:** Are there any supply‑chain or regulatory hurdles that could delay the Bharuch plant’s commercial start?
