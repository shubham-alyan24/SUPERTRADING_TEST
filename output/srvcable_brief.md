# Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd. (NSE: SRVCABLE)  
*As of 23 September 2026*

## Snapshot  
Sarvottam Cables Ltd. manufactures power cables, wires and conductors for utilities, data‑centres and export markets. In Q1 FY27 (Apr‑Jun 2026) revenue rose **18 % to ₹1,248 cr** and profit after tax reached **₹82 cr**; EBITDA margin slipped to **11.2 %** due to a sharp rise in copper prices【3】. The order book grew to **₹3,900 cr** (up from ₹3,150 cr YoY) and the new Bharuch plant, which will add ~30 % capacity, is slated for commercial start‑up in Q3 FY27【3】. Net debt‑to‑equity stands at **0.35×**【2】.

## Bull Case  
- **Robust top‑line growth**: 18 % YoY revenue increase and a swelling order book indicate strong demand from power‑transmission and data‑centre projects【3】.  
- **Capacity expansion**: Bharuch plant will boost output by ~30 % and is already 70 % funded, financed mainly through internal accruals【2】.  
- **Diversifying revenue mix**: Exports now account for 21 % of sales (up from 16 % YoY) and data‑centre cables, currently ~9 % of domestic revenue, are expected to double in two years【3】【2】.  
- **Healthy balance sheet**: Net debt‑to‑equity of 0.35× and modest term‑debt usage suggest financial flexibility【2】.  
- **Low promoter pledge**: As of June 2026 only **4.1 %** of promoter holdings are pledged, a sharp fall from earlier levels, reducing governance risk【8】.  
- **Institutional backing**: FII and DII stakes have risen to 11.8 % and 14.6 % respectively, indicating growing investor confidence【8】.

## Bear Case  
- **Margin pressure**: Copper prices jumped ~14 % in the quarter, pulling operating margin down 170 bps to 11.2 %【3】. Pass‑through of costs may lag due to price‑variation clauses resetting over 1‑2 quarters【2】.  
- **Rising receivable days**: Days sales outstanding climbed from 78 to 96 days, driven by slower payments from large state utilities【2】, potentially stressing cash flow.  
- **Tax litigation risk**: The company faces a GST demand of **₹46.3 cr** for FY2020‑21 & FY2021‑22; while management expects no material impact, the outcome is uncertain【7】.  
- **Execution risk on new plant**: Commercial production is targeted for Q3 FY27; any delays could postpone anticipated capacity‑related earnings uplift【3】.  
- **Historical promoter pledging**: Earlier reports highlighted a rise to 35 % pledged shares in 2024, suggesting past governance concerns that could re‑emerge if pledging climbs again【4】.  

## Open Questions  
- **Revenue figure discrepancy**: A news article reported ₹1,428 cr revenue (35 % growth)【1】, whereas the official filing shows ₹1,248 cr (18 % growth)【3]. Which figure reflects the audited result?  
- **Speed of copper cost pass‑through**: How quickly can the company translate higher input costs into higher prices without eroding demand?  
- **Bharuch plant timeline**: Will the plant achieve commercial production on schedule, and what is the expected contribution to FY27 earnings?  
- **GST demand resolution**: What is the likely timeline and financial impact of the appeal against the ₹46.3 cr GST order?  
- **Future promoter pledge trends**: Will the low pledged percentage observed in June 2026 remain stable, or could it rise again?
